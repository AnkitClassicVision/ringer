# Vintage Optical publish-readiness yardstick

## Product requirement

Produce a defensible publish-candidate package in which every visible number and score has explicit lineage, formula, direction, evidence, limitations, and a plain-language explanation. Preserve the repository's human delivery gate and do not publish or send.

## Current boundary

Project Room status is `inventory_review_required`. All work remains internal-only until Ankit reviews the four-source inventory and the room is promoted to `draftable`.

## Units and proof

- U1 Source and number inventory: enumerate every visible numeric occurrence and classify it as substantive, structural, source identifier, or formula token. Proof: coverage validator reports zero unexplained substantive numbers.
- U2 Formula audit: independently recompute all six headline scores and three specialty scores from canonical bands and weights. Proof: formula validator reports exact equality and valid bands only.
- U3 Logic audit: test scale direction, unknown handling, city-versus-catchment separation, route limitations, peer-tier claims, and Fix Card math. Proof: independent review contains no fatal or material logical inconsistency.
- U4 Explainer: create a readable item-by-item document covering headline scores, raw public facts, route values, specialty scores, Fix Cards, source IDs, assumptions, and disconfirmers. Proof: explainer validator maps every substantive page number to a lineage record and every formula to recomputed inputs.
- U5 Render and review: render the explainer to Letter PDF, inspect visually, and run a fresh report-only publish-readiness review. Proof: technical validators pass; visual QA passes; reviewer names the highest true state.

## Non-goals

- No email, CRM write, upload, outreach, or publication.
- No invented operating, patient, conversion, capacity, collections, or economics values.
- No replacement of missing catchment data with city data.
- No Project Room promotion without human review of the source inventory.

## Decision residue

- Hardest decision: whether directional public-only scores are sufficiently defensible for publication when several canonical inputs are unknown. The audit must treat neutral 50s as explicit unknown handling, not measured facts, and explain every limited 65 or 35 band.
- Rejected alternative: explain only the six headline scores. That would leave route facts, demographics, specialty scores, and Fix Card formulas untraceable.
- Least-confident assumption: the bounded named peer set supports a directional competitive-pressure read despite not being a complete supply census. The explainer must keep that limitation visible.
