### R3. H1 Hypothesis Evaluation & Falsification Probe

- **VERDICT**: **INSUFFICIENT** (Evidence is underdetermined; H1 is consistent with 3 Sunday data points but unverified across non-Sunday run dates).
- **File:Line Evidence**:
  - [contract/TEMPORAL-CONTRACT.md:30-43](file:///home/ankit114/repos/mott-v21-snap/contract/TEMPORAL-CONTRACT.md#L30-L43)
  - [harness/TEMPORAL-BRIEF.md:55-58](file:///home/ankit114/repos/mott-v21-snap/harness/TEMPORAL-BRIEF.md#L55-L58)
- **Analysis**:
  On Sunday 2026-07-26, sending `monday next week` yielded `08/03`, `wednesday next week` yielded `08/05`, and `tuesday next week` yielded `08/04`. H1 posits that the gateway anchors weeks on Sunday (making Sunday 07/26 part of week 07/26–08/01, thus "next week" is 08/02–08/08).  
  However, an alternative hypothesis (H2) exists: the gateway evaluates `X next week` by resolving `X` to its next upcoming occurrence and adding 7 days. On Sunday 07/26, the next upcoming Tuesday is 07/28; adding 7 days yields 08/04. Both H1 and H2 predict identical outputs on Sunday.
- **Cheapest Falsification Probe**:
  - On Monday 2026-07-27, probe **`sunday` vs `sunday next week`**.
  - Under H1 (Sunday-anchored calendar: Mon 07/27 is day 2 of week 07/26–08/01):
    - `sunday` (soonest Sunday) = 08/02.
    - `sunday next week` (Sunday of next week) = 08/09.
  - Under H2 (add 7d to next occurrence):
    - `sunday` (next occurrence) = 08/02.
    - `sunday next week` (08/02 + 7d) = 08/09.
  - To cleanly separate week-boundary logic from 7-day relative offsets on Monday 07/27: Send bare vague phrase **`next week`** to the gateway and inspect the returned range in `result.slots`. If `next week` returns `08/03–08/07`, the gateway uses Monday anchoring; if it returns `08/02–08/08` or `08/10–08/14`, Sunday anchoring or 14-day capping is active.
- **What Would Change Ruling**: Execution of the Monday probe comparing returned slot date ranges for bare and qualified temporal phrases.

- **Analysis & Mitigation**:
  The bounded-harm argument holds because:
  1. Outbound campaigns do not execute on Sundays ([TEMPORAL-CONTRACT.md:59](file:///home/ankit114/repos/mott-v21-snap/contract/TEMPORAL-CONTRACT.md#L59)).
  2. Inbound Sunday queries receiving an offer get explicit calendar dates (e.g., `"08/04/2026 10:30 am"`), preventing patients from silently booking an unexpected date.
  3. Patient confirmation requires explicit affirmation of the dated slot.
- **Interim Mitigation (No Model Math & No Platform Variables Required)**:
  - Enforce explicit date formatting in offer prompts: Require offer nodes to state both day-of-week and full calendar date (e.g., *"Tuesday, August 4th (08/04/2026)"*). This eliminates date ambiguity for inbound Sunday replies without requiring model-side date math.
